<a href="https://estas.univ-gustave-eiffel.fr/english/estas">ESTAS</a> Lab at Université Gustave Eiffel in Lille is offering an exciting Master’s thesis opportunity on the topic of "Connecting Discrete and Continuous Neural Network Models."

🔍 What’s it all about?

We’re working to establish formal relationships between the behavior of discrete neural networks and their continuous counterparts, neural ordinary differential equations (neural ODEs). These relationships could then be used to guarantee the safety of one model after verifying the other, ultimately enabling safer and more reliable AI systems.
